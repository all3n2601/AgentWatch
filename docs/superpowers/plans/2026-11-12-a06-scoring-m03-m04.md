# A06 scoring with sweep-truth-v1 and the split-v3 M03/M04 model — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use subagent-driven-development (recommended) or executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** `score-held-out` reads #74's `sweep-truth-v1` truth folders and scores `rules-v1` and
Rohit's frozen M03 classifier with its M04 calibration on the A06 sweeps, training nothing. It
then reruns once and records the result.

**Architecture:** The agent service loses its own truth command. #74's recorder (`services/agent`)
and rule (`pipelines/data`) produce the truths, and `pipelines/model` consumes them. A new module
`held_out_model.py` loads M03's saved classifier and M04's calibration, checks their identity,
and scores rows. `score_held_out.py` wires truths, rows, both scorers, the leakage check and the
report together. The report module only changes where unscored steps come from.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.13, uv workspace, LightGBM 4.7.0, NumPy, MLflow (local SQLite), pytest,
Ruff.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-a06-held-out-scoring-design.md`, section "Change
after #70, #72 and #74 (2026-11-12)". The sections above it still hold where that section doesn't
replace them.

## Review Focus

1. **The real truth files can't be read by the scorer.** #74's real output has fields the
   synthetic fixtures don't, or lacks fields they assume. Expected: the contract test in Task 2
   reads real `make sweep-truth` output for both frozen sweeps with `load_truths` (skipped
   without the data).
2. **A loaded classifier differs from the one M03 evaluated.** For example a wrong iteration
   count, feature order or class order. Expected: refusal before scoring. Task 3's
   reproduction check compares loaded predictions on the split's validation rows with M03's
   `validation_predictions.jsonl`.
3. **The M03 and M04 folders come from different runs,** or M04 is an unfitted calibration.
   Expected: refusal naming the mismatch, with nothing written. Task 3 has tests.
4. **A truth folder whose `truth.jsonl` was replaced after `truth.json`.** Expected: refusal on
   `truth_sha256`. Task 2 has a test.
5. **Every step of a sweep is unjudged,** for example a sweep with only root and tool steps.
   Expected: the attempt fails as recorded, with "no step could be scored" and the unscored
   counts. It must not crash and must not report rates of 0. Task 4 has a test.

### Task 3: Load the frozen M03 classifier and M04 calibration

**Files:**
- Create: `pipelines/model/src/agentwatch_model/held_out_model.py`
- Create: `pipelines/model/tests/test_held_out_model.py`
- Modify: `pipelines/model/src/agentwatch_model/held_out_scorers.py`. Remove
  `train_classifier`, `classifier_scores` and `TRAINING_SPLITS`. Keep `ScoredStep`,
  `RULES_SCORER`, `rule_scores` and `seen_task_groups`.
- Modify: `pipelines/model/tests/test_held_out_scorers.py`. Remove the tests for the deleted
  functions.

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes:
  - `agentwatch_model.candidates.check_lightgbm`;
  - `agentwatch_model.features.FEATURES` and `feature_matrix`;
  - `agentwatch_model.calibrate.score_test` and `read_json`;
  - `agentwatch_model.calibration_report.Fit`;
  - `agentwatch_model.train_candidates.MODEL_FILES` and `PREPROCESSING`;
  - `agentwatch_model.rule_baseline.split_identity`.
- Produces:
  - `class HeldOutModelError(ValueError)`.
  - A frozen dataclass `FrozenClassifier` with fields `booster`, `class_order: tuple[str, ...]`,
    `temperature: float`, `threshold: float` and `identity: dict`.
  - `load_frozen_classifier(candidates_dir: Path, calibrated_dir: Path, split: Path) ->
    FrozenClassifier`.
  - `check_reproduces_validation(model: FrozenClassifier, validation_rows: list[dict],
    candidates_dir: Path) -> None`.
  - `frozen_scores(model: FrozenClassifier, rows: list[dict]) -> list[ScoredStep]`, with scorer
    name `CLASSIFIER_SCORER = "lightgbm-classifier-m03-m04"`.

`load_frozen_classifier` raises `HeldOutModelError`, naming the file and field, unless all of
these hold:
- `candidates_dir/report.json` has `status == "evaluated"`;
- its `split` equals `split_identity(split)`, by file name and SHA-256;
- its `contract` is `{"version": "eval-contract-v3", "hash": "1cab0cbe", "status": "accepted"}`;
- the SHA-256 of `classifier.txt` equals both `report.json`'s `model_files.classifier` and
  `calibrated_dir/calibration.json`'s `model_files.classifier.sha256`;
- `preprocessing.json`'s SHA-256 equals `report.json`'s `preprocessing.sha256`;
- `validation_predictions.jsonl`'s SHA-256 equals `report.json`'s
  `validation_predictions.sha256`;
- `preprocessing.json`'s `features` equals `list(FEATURES)`, and its `probability_columns`
  equals its `class_order`;
- `calibration.json` has `fitted is True` and `status == "evaluated"`, its `training_version`
  equals `preprocessing.json`'s, and its `temperature` and `abstention_threshold` are finite
  and greater than 0.

`identity` records the training version, calibration version, contract, split, the SHA-256 of
`classifier.txt`, `preprocessing.json` and `calibration.json`, the temperature and the threshold.
The booster is `lightgbm.Booster(model_file=…)`. M03 saved it at its best iteration, so it
predicts with every iteration.

`frozen_scores` takes probabilities from `booster.predict(feature_matrix(rows))` in
`class_order`. It applies `score_test` with
`Fit(fitted=True, temperature=…, threshold=…, reason=None, validation_steps=0, usable_steps=0,
temperature_at_bound=False, selection=None, early_stopping_on_fit_split=False)`. Each
`ScoredStep` has the top class, the calibrated confidence and the abstention flag.

`check_reproduces_validation` predicts the split's validation rows and compares them with
`validation_predictions.jsonl`, joined on (`run_id`, `step_id`). It raises `HeldOutModelError`
when:
- a row is missing on either side;
- a prediction differs;
- any probability differs by more than `REPRODUCTION_TOLERANCE = 1e-9`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests** in `test_held_out_model.py`. The fixture is synthetic:
  train a candidate on `training_fixtures.dataset`, save it with `save_models`, and write a
  matching `report.json`, `preprocessing.json`, `validation_predictions.jsonl` and
  `calibration.json`.
  - `test_a_matching_pair_loads_and_records_its_identity`
  - `test_loaded_scores_equal_the_trained_candidate`: the loaded and in-memory predictions agree
    to within `REPRODUCTION_TOLERANCE` on every row.
  - `test_abstains_below_the_calibrated_threshold`: with `threshold=0.99`, every step abstains;
    with `threshold=1e-6`, none does.
  - Parametrised refusal tests, each asserting that the error names the field:
    - another split file;
    - contract status `proposed`;

    - `fitted: false`;
    - a changed preprocessing `features` order;
    - a non-finite temperature;
    - a `training_version` mismatch.
  - `test_a_validation_prediction_that_differs_is_refused`: one probability shifted by 1e-6.

- Produces: the CLI `python -m agentwatch_model.score_held_out --truth DIR… --sweep-root DIR

  - The defaults are `.data/candidates` and `.data/calibration`. `--training` and
    `--calibration` are removed.

Behaviour:
- `run_attempt` loads the frozen classifier first. A `HeldOutModelError` there exits 2 as a
  setup error, like a contract error.
- It loads the split's train, validation and test rows through `load_split`, with the leakage
  check on, then runs `check_reproduces_validation`.
- A function `scored_overlap(rows: list[dict], split_rows: list[dict]) -> list[str]` lists
  every scored `run_id`, and every scored non-null `plan_sha256`, that appears in any split
  row. The attempt fails as recorded, writing no predictions, when that list isn't empty.
- `seen_task_groups` comes from the train rows.
- `input_hashes` adds the SHA-256 of `classifier.txt`, `preprocessing.json`, the candidates
  `report.json`, `validation_predictions.jsonl` and `calibration.json`.
